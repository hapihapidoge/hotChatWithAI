## Question

Claude output now contains watermarks . How can I remove text watermarks from Claude output?

## Answer

In my opinion, the term watermark is misleading and not really appropriate. A legal requirement to label AI-generated content does not automatically mean that removing a specific Claude watermark is itself a criminal offense or generally illegal. In the EU, for text, the transparency requirements for a person publishing AI-generated content apply in particular to AI-generated or manipulated texts concerning matters of public interest, where there has been no human review or editorial control. In very simple terms: If you have sufficiently modified and reviewed the text yourself, stand behind the final version, and take responsibility for it, then an AI disclosure may no longer be required.
