## Answer

By having the model perform far faster and cheaper on TPU compared to GPU, enough that it doesn't make economic sense (except for those banned from renting/buying TPU chips) to use GPU. I don't buy this argument though; Google is in this position because they had been using machine learning at larger scale far earlier, if there's enough profit to gain others would be designing their own chips. Gemini's advantages compared to open-weight models released by Chinese companies are also less significant outside the CCP-enforced censorship. Google being an American company means any company (even in the US) might lose access to GCP due to random Executive Order anytime and would prefer a more reliable partner.
