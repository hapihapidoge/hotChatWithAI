## Question

Are there separate usage limits for Gemini image generation, independent of the daily usage limits for Gemini chat?

## Answer

Image generation in Gemini has its own usage limits, depending on the specific feature. These are separate from the general Gemini chat and model limits. The exact limits depend on your Google account type, subscription, and the image generation model you use. There are differences in image generation usage limits between school and work accounts, depending on the specific Google Workspace license.
