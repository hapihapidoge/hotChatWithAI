## Question

I heard that models will no longer be better in quality starting from next year or so due to "model collapse", as Ai-generated content has "polluted" the internet so much, causing models to no longer have quality content to train on. Is this true?

## Answer

Let's break your question up into subparts. Training on AI generated data leads to model collapse The Internet is polluted with AI data Therefore models will no longer be able to improve in the near future. Regarding claim 1, this is largely seen as true. The canonical source of this is a Nature article from 2024: "AI models collapse when trained on recursively generated data" However, this article has sparked a debate, and is largely empirical, so we should not view it has having fully closed the issue. Particularly, the recent rise of Recursive Self Improvement indicates this may not be true in the long term (depending on how exactly you define AI generated data). Regarding claim 2, this claim is pretty nonsensical. Even if most Internet content is AI generated, it's not like therefore we have lost access to the older parts of the Internet that are not. There is a more intelligent version of this claim that goes: AI models will stop improving because the useful parts of the Internet have already been used to saturation. Perhaps this is what you meant. But that leads to claim 3. Claim 3 is certainly false. Even if we never got more data, improvements in reasoning and other algorithmic advances will drive AI forward regardless.
