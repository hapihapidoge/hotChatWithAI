## Question

Many neural network-based music identifiers (that are sometimes integrated into LLMs) are trained exclusively on freely licensed music (or public-domain music) to avoid liability. Is there a "RAG-like" funcionality for this?

## Answer

Can neural music identifiers trained on free data still identify non-free songs? Yes. Example: Segment Length Matters: A Study of Segment Lengths on Audio Fingerprinting Performance . (If the test songs were included in the training phase, the evaluation may measure memorization rather than the model's true capability to generalize its acoustic feature extraction to new music.)
