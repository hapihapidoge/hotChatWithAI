## Question

I have an available banked reset and have enabled Allow your assistant to use resets in Codex. The documentation says the relevant usage allowance must have 10% or less remaining. Does this also work at 0%, or do I need to ask before the allowance is exhausted?

## Answer

In my attempt at 0%, the task returned a usage-limit message before it could redeem the reset. I had to redeem it manually in Usage settings.
